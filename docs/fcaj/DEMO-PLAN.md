# Demo and oral-defense plan / Kế hoạch demo và giải thích

Use fictional shop policies and your own two test accounts. Personally perform/record the flow; do not use a seeded QA answer as evidence of model generation.

1. Explain the real shop-support problem and what the app deliberately cannot do (order status/refund execution/multiple shops).
2. Show sign-in, owner PDF upload/processing, a real cited answer and a missing-information refusal; point to source/page and source-scope choices.
3. Demonstrate the second account cannot read the first account's PDF/history, including with the administrator role.
4. Rate a tracked answer helpful, change reason/rating and withdraw. Show aggregate counters and export; explain that privacy is preserved and citation rate is not truth accuracy.
5. Send a support question with explicit consent, handle it in staff UI, and show why private PDFs/history were not silently attached.
6. Show draft versus publication, current shop contacts, audit, queue/alarm snapshots and deletion completion evidence.
7. Explain your own architecture diagram, cost assumptions, retries, failure cases and which work was assisted. State what was not tested (e.g. load/restore/email/visual flows as applicable).

Prepare a short answer for: why serverless/SQS/int8 vectors; why the parser is isolated; which data crosses regions; what is atomic/idempotent; how a deleting user is blocked; what the quotas do and do not cap; how to reproduce and safely clean a personal lab.

No blog posting, workshop publishing, attendance record or event photo was generated/sent on your behalf. Capture only consented data and remove credentials/tokens/presigned URLs from video/screenshots.
